# Attendance usability testing

## Aim

Check whether students and lecturers can understand the tap process, find attendance information and recover from a missed tap without confusion.

## Participants

Recruit 3 to 5 volunteers. Use fictional student records during testing. Do not ask participants to share real student numbers or passwords.

## Tasks

1. Sign in using a sample role.
2. Find the correct module and session.
3. Simulate a Tap IN and Tap OUT.
4. Find the attendance record.
5. Report a missing tap from the student view.
6. Review the query and record a manual correction from the lecturer view.

## What to observe

Note where people hesitate, whether labels make sense, how many attempts each task takes and whether the system explains errors clearly. Ask participants what they would change.
